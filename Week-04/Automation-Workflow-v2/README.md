# FL-04 — Ship an Automation Workflow v2

## Objective

Build a no-code AI workflow that automates source-grounded research into structured study notes.

---

## Workflow

Research Topic
        ↓
NotebookLM
        ↓
Source-grounded Summary
        ↓
ChatGPT Processing
        ↓
Structured Notes
        ↓
Human Review
        ↓
Markdown Export

---

## Tools

- NotebookLM
- ChatGPT
- GitHub
- Markdown

---

## Workflow Steps

### Step 1
Research with NotebookLM

### Step 2
Generate source-grounded summary

### Step 3
Convert into structured notes using ChatGPT

### Step 4
Review and export

---

## Results

| Topic | Workflow Time |
|--------|---------------|
| Prompt Engineering | 6 min |
| RAG | 7 min |
| AI Agents | 6 min |
| MCP | 7 min |
| Vector Databases | 6 min |

Average workflow time: ~6.5 minutes

---

## Manual vs Workflow

Manual Research
≈60 minutes

Workflow

---

## Human Review

- Verify technical facts
- Remove duplicate ideas
- Improve explanations
- Add personal insights

---

## Failure Points

- Limited by uploaded sources
- AI may oversimplify concepts
- Human review required before publishing
