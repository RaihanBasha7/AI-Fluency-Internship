## Reviewer Questions

The following questions were used during the review:

1. What happens when users provide unexpected input?
2. Can every important link and navigation path be completed successfully?
3. Does the website remain usable on smaller screens?
4. Are failures communicated clearly to users?
5. Can a recruiter easily understand who the portfolio owner is and what they do?
6. Is the site discoverable and shareable?
7. Are remaining limitations documented honestly?
