## Testing Approach

I deliberately tested my portfolio outside the normal happy path to identify failures and weak points.

The testing included:

- Empty and invalid form submissions
- Garbage and unexpected input
- Rapid repeated actions
- Navigation and external links
- Responsive behavior
- Different browsers and devices
- Page refresh and navigation behavior
- Basic SEO and discoverability
- Performance testing
