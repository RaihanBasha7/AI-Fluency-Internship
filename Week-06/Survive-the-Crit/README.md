# Survive the Crit — Week 6

## Purpose

This folder contains the evidence for the Week 6 **Survive the Crit** checkpoint of the AI Fluency Internship.

The goal of this checkpoint was to get an honest second-person review of the live portfolio, identify usability and credibility problems, separate **must-fixes** from **nice-to-haves**, and implement the must-fixes on the live site.

## Deliverables

- `reviewer-feedback.md` — reviewer's feedback, classification, changes, and verification
- `before/` — evidence from before the fixes
- `after/` — evidence from after the fixes

## Review process

The reviewer was asked first:

1. In ten seconds, what do I do?
2. Would you believe I am good at it?

The remaining feedback was collected without defending the original design.

## Classification

### Must-fix
A problem is a must-fix when it is:
- confusing,
- broken,
- harmful to the portfolio's primary action,
- or prevents the portfolio's proof from landing.

### Nice-to-have
A nice-to-have is an improvement that can be postponed without materially hurting understanding, credibility, or the primary action.

## Portfolio proof statement

> I build production-ready AI systems that turn messy real-world problems into reliable products.

## Final verification

After the must-fixes were implemented, the live portfolio was checked again to confirm that the changes were actually deployed and working.
